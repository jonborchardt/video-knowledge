# Audience report

What YouTube Studio says about the public videos and Shorts, read by hand from its exports; one dated section per pull.
Numbers come from Studio's Advanced-mode exports (Content table, Lifetime; the audience-retention chart per video), never
from an API call, and they never reach the evidence, a script or a verdict. Nothing here is automated.

**Reading guidelines.** Every available number is shown. Under 100 views a percentage is a few viewers' behaviour and
is read as such, not as a trend; under 1,000 impressions a click-through rate is noise and is marked so; audience
retention is read only where Studio drew the curve (it draws none under a minimum of views). A handful of views on every output are our own checks after a launch. A finding is a drop that lines up with
a chapter or scene start; a curve that only slopes is the normal shape of any video and says nothing by itself.

## Structure to read the curves against

Chapter starts are the description's chapters (`video/review/review.json`); scene starts are the timeline's
(`video/timeline.json`). Neither script carried a pacing warning.

The channel total for the range is 40 views and 13.9 minutes: the other 6 views (2026-10-25, 11-02, 11-03) are on
outputs not in the table, the fixture's unlisted uploads and their checks. Zero subscribers gained.

**When the views came.** Each output's views sit on its launch day and the next: the first video 2 + 6 + 1 on
2026-10-28 to 10-30 and none since; the four of 2026-11-04 took 18 views that day, 7 the next, 0 on 11-06. That pattern is
consistent with our own post-launch checks (open, confirm, close) making up most of the views; the export cannot tell
ours from a visitor's. The 25 s and 9 s average durations on eight- and seven-minute videos are averages over six and
nine views: they say the typical view was short, not how far any one viewer got.

**Impressions.** 873 thumbnail impressions over two weeks, 0.57% clicked. Under the 1,000 guideline and spread over
five outputs, the rates (0% to 4.17%) are one or two clicks each and say nothing about the thumbnails or titles; the
count itself says YouTube is showing the videos to a few hundred people and almost none click.

**Enough traffic to read anything?** No. 34 views, an unknown but probably large share of them ours, no retention
curve, one or two clicks per output.

**Drop-offs the structure explains?** Not readable: without a curve there is no drop-off to place against the chapter
and scene starts above.

**Anything to do differently?** Nothing the data supports. The only external number of any size is 873 impressions at
0.57%, and two weeks of a new channel with five uploads is too little to call that a title or thumbnail problem.

**Decision (Jon, 2026-11-07): Plan G closed.** The numbers are recorded; no further pull is scheduled and nothing is
built. A later hand pull makes sense only once there are more videos and some traffic that is not ours.
