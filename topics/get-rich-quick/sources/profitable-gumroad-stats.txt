How Much Do Gumroad Products Actually Make?
Earnings are steeply top-heavy. Around one product in forty has ever recorded a sale, and among the ones that do sell, the median grosses under $100 across its entire life. The revenue distribution above shows the rest of the shape: a thin band of hits takes most of the money while the long tail earns close to nothing. If you are deciding whether to build a digital product, plan for the median outcome rather than the screenshot on someone's launch thread.
What Should a Digital Product Cost?
Gumroad is a low-price marketplace. The median paid product sells for about $7, and most paid listings sit under ten dollars. Roughly a tenth of the catalog is free outright and a quarter is pay-what-you-want, so a large share is priced at or near zero on purpose, usually to build a mailing list rather than to earn. If you are pricing a first product, buyers here are already anchored to the sub-$10 band, and the price distribution above shows exactly where the mass sits.
Which Digital Product Types Earn the Most?
Ebooks and generic digital files dominate the catalog by count, but they are not what earns. Among products that sell, bundles and courses clear several times what an ebook does, while memberships add recurring revenue across a small slice of the market. That trade-off is the whole point: the crowded, cheap formats are hardest to stand out in, and the higher-effort formats clear more money against far less competition. The product-type breakdown above shows how common each format is set against what it tends to earn.
Gumroad Statistics FAQ
How much money do Gumroad products make?
Earnings are highly concentrated. About 2.4% of Gumroad products have a recorded sale, and among those that do sell the median product grosses roughly $89 over its lifetime. More than half of selling products take in under $100, and only about 0.4% of all products ever clear $1,000. A small set of hits captures most of the revenue.
What percentage of Gumroad creators actually make money?
Across the products Profitable tracks, about 2.4% have a recorded sale and roughly 11.5% carry at least one rating, which implies at least one buyer. Only around 0.4% of all products earn more than $1,000. So while getting started is easy, meaningful income is rare and concentrated in a small minority of products.
What is the average price of a Gumroad product?
The median paid Gumroad product costs $7, and close to 60% of paid products are priced under $10. On top of that, about 10% of the catalog is free outright and another 25% is pay-what-you-want, so a large share is priced at or near zero by choice.
Which type of digital product sells best on Gumroad?
By count the catalog is dominated by ebooks and generic digital files, but courses and bundles earn the most per product: median revenue among earners is about $156 for courses and $210 for bundles, versus roughly $35 for ebooks. Higher-effort formats face less competition and tend to clear more money.
How many products are on Gumroad?
Profitable tracks 4M+ active Gumroad products from more than 1.2M sellers across 11 product types, from ebooks and courses to memberships, bundles, and physical goods. Annual product volume has climbed steeply, and the publishing-by-year chart above shows how much of the catalog arrived recently.
