On May 29, 2026, the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) issued updated program guidance for the Home
Energy Rebates programs, which includes changes that impact Energy Saver North Carolina.

DOE’s updated guidance for HOMES and HEAR (Program Notice 26-1 and Program Notice 26-2) must be
implemented by Sept. 1, 2026. To meet these requirements, multiple program changes will take effect.

Major HEAR Updates

Fuel Switching Projects are NOT allowed: Rebates will no longer be allowed for the replacement of
gas or any other fossil fuel appliances through the HEAR rebates pathway.

Electric-to-Electric Upgrades ARE allowed: HEAR rebates are now permitted to replace existing
electric appliances with more efficient electric equipment

New Insulation and Air Sealing Standards: Insulation and air sealing upgrades may be required
alongside a HEAR heat pump-related project.

Beginning Sept. 1, 2026, all existing and new HEAR applications that have not yet reached rebate
reservation status must comply with the new guidance. Energy Saver NC contractors and the
application processing team will continue to support applicants to ensure a smooth transition with
the updated federal requirements.

What does this mean for applicants?

Current and new HEAR applicants should continue applying, even if their home has fossil fuel
systems. Submit all required information so your application can continue moving forward. The
application processing team and your contractor will explain which upgrades are possible based on
your existing systems.

During the application phase, you do not need to know whether your home needs air sealing or
insulation upgrades. Contractors and the application processing team will guide you through these
standards and requirements.

Submitting a complete application helps the process move faster.

The Energy Saver North Carolina program is updating materials and content to reflect these changes.
