The ellipsis on the second line is the elision, so the last line is read as the tail of the same sentence and not as a second quotation. Earlier lines stay lit; nothing dims, nothing moves after the third line lands. The speaker is named only in the citation block beneath, never set as part of the quotation.

**On screen.** ""technically these heat" / ""pump tax credits ..." / ""available through 2033""

**On-screen citations.** The Thought Provocateur

**Direction.** Cold open: the quotation mark is already setting as the first word lands. The narration spends its first four seconds dating the quotation ('In an earlier video ...'), so hold the band empty but for the quotation mark until the quoted words begin; then build line 1 on 'technically', line 2 on 'pump tax credits' and line 3 on 'available'. If a line fails to align to its words, pin it with a line:N cue in render.json rather than letting the tail appear before it is spoken. Hold all three lines through the pause.
