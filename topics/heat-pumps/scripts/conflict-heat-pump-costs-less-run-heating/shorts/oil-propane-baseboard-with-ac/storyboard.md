# Storyboard: Oil, propane or electric heat, plus AC? A national model says 95 to 100 percent of homes save.

6 scenes, 1:20 at 150 wpm including pauses. Script 84774b08fe (passed). Storyboard status: **ok**.

## 1. sc01 (0:00 to 0:15, 14.9s incl. 0.5s pause)

**Purpose.** Land the figure first: the share of oil, propane and electric homes with AC that the model says save.

**Narration** (s01). 95 to 100 percent: the share of homes heating mainly with oil, propane or electricity, and already running air conditioning, that a national study estimates would pay lower bills with a heat pump.

**Visual** (data-chart). One stat tile centred in the portrait frame; the figure 95%–100% lands on "95" and holds while the sentence names the homes it covers.

## 3. sc03 (0:21 to 0:34, 13.3s incl. 0.5s pause)

**Purpose.** Say plainly that the figure is modelled, not metered.

**Narration** (s03). It is a model: the study simulated the bills of such homes across the country, so the figure is an estimate of what bills would be, not a reading from anyone's meter.

**Visual** (typography). Three short lines build down the frame as the sentence moves: it is a model, the bills were simulated, nobody's meter was read.

**On screen.** "A model" / "Simulated bills" / "Not a meter reading"

**On-screen citations.** Wilson 2024 (Wilson 2024 (NREL and LBNL), heat pumps for all?, lines 393-L401)

**Direction.** Lines on "model", "simulated" and "meter"; hold on the last.
