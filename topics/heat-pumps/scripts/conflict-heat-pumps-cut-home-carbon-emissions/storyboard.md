## 15. sc15 (3:54 to 4:31, 37.4s incl. 1s pause)

**Purpose.** Give the answer in three lines.

**Narration** (s18, s19, s20). So does swapping gas for a heat pump cut emissions on today's grid? In the British homes, yes, by about a third of their energy footprint, and in RMI's words it already cuts carbon on today's grid in all but the most coal-heavy systems, though that was the grid when RMI wrote it. The exception is a grid like Chicago's. How rare that exception is, the evidence can only estimate: the national American studies both build in a grid that gets cleaner, so neither is a reading of today's grid alone.
