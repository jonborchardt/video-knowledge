## Why the answer keeps changing [heading:hook fp=d07e51be72]

In January 2026 a gallon of regular gas cost well below what it had averaged across Joe Biden's presidency; by spring it cost well above that average. [s01 ok fp=2da1e305b2] So which is true now? [s02 fp=1880d2d33f] The answer turns on which month you call now, and the federal price record shows exactly when it flipped, and how the wider measure of prices compares. [s03 fp=d2a901def2]

## Is gas cheaper now than it was under Biden? [heading:question-1 fp=0fce1f443b]

At a cabinet meeting President Trump put the case bluntly: "It was $4 and $5 for a gallon of gas", he said, adding "And now it's probably 225". [s04 ok fp=6b61520e01] A few months later CBS News was describing pump prices as "at their highest point since mid-2022". [s06 ok fp=d3a5e0aacf] The Energy Information Administration's monthly record of regular gasoline, in dollars not adjusted for inflation, puts Biden's term in context: from February 2021 through January 2025 the price averaged $3.46 a gallon and peaked at $4.93 in June 2022. [s07 ok fp=aeb6b37dbc] Every month of 2025 came in under that average, December lowest at $2.89, though the year was about level with the final months of his term rather than below them. [s08 ok fp=a1f6e558c3] January 2026 was lower again, at $2.81. [s09 ok fp=783d7bc20d]

[s11 ok fp=8be7b960d2] Reporters linked the climb to the war with Iran: MS NOW said gas was "up by well over a dollar since the war started", and NBC News said "it's the war in Iran that's largely causing fuel prices to rise". **unresolved (inflation-2026/iran-conflict-has-materially-raised-gasoline):** What the weekly record establishes is the timing of the jump in March 2026; a price series on its own cannot rule out causes other than the conflict, so the link rests on timing. [s12 ok fp=08d1c3f7c8]

In October the president made the same argument about crude: "Oil was higher under Biden than it is under Trump". [s14 ok fp=5ab8d3c98f] The EIA's crude table says otherwise: West Texas Intermediate averaged $97 a barrel in September 2026, its latest month, and no month of Biden's term was higher except March through July 2022. [s15 ok fp=96eaba216a]

[s16 ok fp=9731fd6dd0] A guest on PBS NewsHour gave the usual explanation, that the pump price "is set in a global market". [s17 ok fp=51f79016e4]

**unresolved (inflation-2026/gasoline-prices-mainly-follow-global-oil):** Prices that rise and fall together fit that explanation, but they cannot show that policy had no part, because policy can move the price of crude itself. [s18 ok fp=d6dc81a53e]

So the honest answer depends on the date: gas was cheaper than the Biden-era average through February 2026, has been dearer in every month since March, and has stayed below the June 2022 peak throughout. [s19 ok fp=3c17507a61] Gas, though, is a single price, and the same argument is made about prices in general. [s20 fp=3bab93d6d7]

## Have prices risen less under this administration than under the last one? [heading:question-2 fp=99e9ae7bfa]

The YouTube channel Squirrelly Economics framed it in totals: "Prices rose about 21% over Biden's 4 years". [s21 ok fp=17d3ddd054] The Bureau of Labor Statistics' consumer price index bears out the totals: it rose 21.4 percent from January 2021 to January 2025, and 6.1 percent from January 2025 to September 2026. [s22 ok fp=65c6a8e24d]

**qualification (inflation-2026/cumulative-price-increases-under-current-administration):** Those totals compare a completed term with a term still running, and the second stretch is much shorter than the first. [s23 ok fp=61c6059d31] Over windows of the current term's length at each end of Biden's term, the index rose 13.5 percent from January 2021 to September 2022 and 4.5 percent from May 2023 to January 2025. [s24 ok fp=8d8aec345a] And the current term's figure is still growing: the twelve-month rate went from 2.4 percent in February 2026 to 3.7 percent in September. [s25 ok fp=a5a948591c]

Figure decision-is-gas-cheaper-than-under-biden/sc22: The yearly rate of consumer price inflation in early and late 2026. [decision-is-gas-cheaper-than-under-biden/sc22 fp=79be1060ab]

So prices have risen more slowly this term than across Biden's term as a whole, though faster than in its last stretch, and by a narrower margin than the totals imply; the index itself cannot say how much of either rise any president caused. [s26 ok fp=9f76dbf19d]

## How to check the next price claim [heading:close fp=a2a255bc9d]

Each answer here came down to the calendar: which month counts as now, and how long a stretch is measured. [s27 fp=17279abbdd] When someone says prices are lower, ask lower than when, and over how long; and when the pump price moves, look at what crude oil did that month. [s28 fp=b27911c69f]
