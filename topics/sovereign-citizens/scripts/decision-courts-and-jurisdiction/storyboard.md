## 19. sc19 (3:59 to 4:37, 38.2s incl. 1s pause)

**Purpose.** Show what the act of 1871 actually did and how briefly that government lasted.

**Narration** (s25, s26, s27). The act of 1871 is real, and the Supreme Court later described what it did: it made the District of Columbia "a body corporate for municipal purposes", a local government that could make contracts, sue and be sued, and look after its streets. The Court called the District "a mere municipal corporation", and said the sovereign power over it was lodged "in the government of the United States". And the government that the 1871 act set up did not even last: an act of 1874 abolished it.
