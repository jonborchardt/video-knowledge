National Do Not Call Registry Data Book for Fiscal Year 2025 - Federal Trade Commission, December 2025

Annual government statistical release on the National Do Not Call Registry: registration counts, telemarketer access, and consumer complaints by topic, call type (live caller vs. robocall) and state, for fiscal year 2025 (October 2024-September 2025).

The National Do Not Call Registry
The National Do Not Call Registry lets consumers tell companies they don't want most telemarketing sales calls. Robocalls that are sales calls are illegal whether or not a number is on the Registry, unless the caller has the consumer's written permission. Since 2003 the Registry has grown to 258,515,050 active registrations as of September 30, 2025. Telemarketers and sellers must remove registered numbers from their call lists at least every 31 days. Consumers report unwanted calls to the FTC at donotcall.gov; the FTC verifies a live-caller complaint against the Registry before accepting it (a robocall complaint is accepted regardless of registration status, since those calls are illegal either way).

Active registrations by fiscal year: 244,302,202 (2021); 246,820,600 (2022); 249,498,621 (2023); 253,721,138 (2024); 258,515,050 (2025).

Complaints by call type and fiscal year (live caller / robocall / total): FY2021: 1,844,210 live caller, 3,427,949 robocall (997,398 other/unreported removed from total), total about 5,006,801 (most were robocalls). FY2022: 889,891 live caller, 2,118,808 robocall. FY2023: 730,159 live caller, 1,157,748 robocall. FY2024: 764,138 live caller, 1,099,229 robocall. FY2025: 802,144 live caller, 1,601,611 robocall, 214,322 call-type-not-reported.

FY2025 complaints by topic (not every complainant reports a topic; the chart's bar-label values, in descending order of scale): Reducing debt (largest category, in the hundreds of thousands); Imposters; Medical & prescriptions; Energy, solar & utilities; Home improvement & cleaning; Warranties & protection plans: 10,813 live-caller complaints and 10,503 robocall complaints; Vacation & timeshares; Lotteries, prizes & sweepstakes; Computer & technical support; Money-making opportunities; Home security & alarms. "Warranties & protection plans" is one of the FTC's standard reportable complaint topics, tracked every fiscal year alongside imposter scams, debt-reduction pitches and timeshare pitches; its two values split almost evenly between live-caller and robocall complaints, unlike several larger topics that skew toward one call type.


DERIVED COMPARISONS
- Active Do Not Call registrations grew from 244.3 million (FY2021) to 258.5 million (FY2025), a gain of about 14.2 million (5.8%) over four years, while total DNC complaints fell from about 5.3 million (FY2021) to about 2.4 million (FY2025) -- registrations keep climbing even as complaint volume roughly halved.
- In FY2025, robocall complaints (1,601,611) outnumbered live-caller complaints (802,144) by about 2 to 1 -- illegal robocalls, not calls from registered numbers' legitimate telemarketers, drive most complaint volume, consistent with registration not stopping calls that are already illegal.
- "Warranties & protection plans" drew 10,813 live-caller complaints and 10,503 robocall complaints in FY2025 (21,316 total) -- a smaller slice than the largest topics (reducing debt, imposters, medical & prescriptions), but a recurring, explicitly tracked FTC complaint category every fiscal year, split almost evenly between live-caller and robocall complaints where most other topics skew to one call type.
