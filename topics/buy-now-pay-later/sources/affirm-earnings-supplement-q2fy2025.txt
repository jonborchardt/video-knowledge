DERIVED COMPARISONS
Affirm's self-reported 30+ day delinquency rate on its US installment-loan book has run in roughly the 0.8%-2.9% range every quarter since FY2019, versus Fed SHED's nationally representative finding that 26% of BNPL users were late on a payment at some point in the prior year, and LendingTree's self-reported survey finding 47% of BNPL users paid late in the past year. These are not the same measure (Affirm's is a point-in-time share of loan balances 30+ days past due on one lender's book; the survey figures are the share of users who were ever late, across providers, in a year) but the gap between a low-single-digit loan-level delinquency rate and a self-reported "ever late" rate near half of users is large enough to note.
