Resolution Criteria

This question will resolve as Yes if, before January 1 of the listed year, a non-test nuclear weapon is detonated anywhere in the world. A deliberate, inadvertent, or accidental/unauthorised detonation would result in the question resolving as Yes. To qualify, the nuclear weapon must be detonated less than 100 kilometers above Earth's mean sea level.

Fine Print

For the purposes of this question, a "nuclear weapon" is a bomb which uses a nuclear fission or fusion reaction as its primary energy source, excluding conventional bombs which spread radioactive fallout (so called "dirty bombs").

Background Info

Since the first detonation of an atomic bomb during the Trinity Test in July of 1945 and the first hostile uses against Hiroshima and Nagasaki in August of that year, the risk of additional nuclear strikes has been of constant concern and speculation. Nuclear weapons stockpiles rapidly rose following their invention, but have since declined from their peak in the late 80s. While four countries have previously possessed nuclear weapons and later gave up possession, there are currently nine countries believed to possess nuclear weapons, and conflicts with and between these countries pose some risk of the use of nuclear weapons.
