## 2. Institution registry

### 01. Commission on Spending

- DECIDED remit: homeowner and consumer economics, big purchases, energy costs, tradeoffs, real savings, cost-benefit
  analysis, consumer financial claims.
- First planned investigations: heat pumps and household energy economics.
- Formal name: Commission on Sensible Expenditure. YouTube: Commonwealth of Reason: Commission on Spending; @CORSpending.
- Series candidate: Sounds Good on Paper (proposed, not a mandatory show title).
- Personality: impossibly thorough, fiscally prudent, procedural, confident, faintly ancient.
- Signature question: what does it actually cost, and under what conditions is it worth it?
- Do not merge MLM/scam episodes simply because money is involved; those go to the Bureau of Dubious Returns.

### 02. Bureau of Dubious Returns

- DECIDED remit: MLMs, get-rich-quick schemes, deceptive promises, doubtful profit claims, dubious business economics.
- Series candidate: Catch Included.
- Personality, PROPOSED: exacting financial examiner; dry skepticism without prejudging an allegation.
- Typical device: claims versus realized outcomes, income-disclosure evidence, probability tables, fees, exclusions.
- Evidence caution: do not label people or companies fraudulent absent support; distinguish illegality, structural
  disadvantage, misleading statements, and poor economics.
- Source inventory: the existing get-rich-quick work.

### 03. Office of Code Pending

- DECIDED remit: agentic software engineering, coding tools, developer workflows, AI-generated code quality, software
  maintainability, engineering outcomes.
- Historical working full name: Office of Computational Affairs. Series: OPEN.
- Personality, PROPOSED: civil-service standards office with code review as administrative procedure.
- Typical device: reproducible benchmarks, engineering tradeoffs, code excerpts, experiment logs, evaluation scores.
- Evidence caution: distinguish benchmarks, anecdotes, vendor claims, and controlled experiments.

### 04. Plausibility Commission

- DECIDED remit: testable scientific claims, nutrition and health research, consumer science, misleading
  interpretations of studies.
- Historical series candidate: Plausibility Department (confusingly close to the institution name, so OPEN).
- Personality, PROPOSED: formally skeptical review panel, eager to establish what evidence can and cannot support.
- Typical device: study-quality hierarchy, confidence intervals, dose and effect-size graphics, replication records.
- Evidence caution: never present general research as individualized medical advice; show strength, limitations, and
  date of evidence.

### 05. Bureau of Public Beliefs

- DECIDED remit: social beliefs, fringe movements, popular misconceptions, unusual convictions, how ideas spread.
- Historical working name: Bureau of Public Convictions. Series candidate: Commonly Untrue.
- Personality, PROPOSED: a registrar of improbable convictions, curious rather than contemptuous.
- Typical device: timeline, historical documents, survey data, claim networks, source-tracing maps.
- Evidence caution: never ridicule a person's identity or invent reasons a group believes something; separate
  documented beliefs from explanatory theories.
- Source inventory: the sovereign-citizen investigations.

### 06. Society of Synthetic Arts

- DECIDED remit: generative music, audiovisual experiments, synthetic performance, creative processes, art made with
  computational systems.
- Series candidate: Pattern Behavior.
- Personality, PROPOSED: a distinguished arts academy holding elaborate exhibitions of algorithmic art.
- Typical device: performance program, score sheet, sonic motif, workshop notebook, moving visual composition.
- Editorial distinction: not every work is an investigative report. Performances may be commissioned works,
  exhibitions, recordings, or proceedings, with the same provenance and attribution rules.
- Existing project context: agentic music and music-video generation, including Strudel-based composition
  (`music/`).
