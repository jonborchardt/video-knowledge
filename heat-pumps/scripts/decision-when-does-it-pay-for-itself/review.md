A viewer will believe buying at replacement lifts the odds above these shares; the model says the shares already are that case. The cited passage does not carry the baseline, so the fix needs evidence first.
- s17 (s17.a) omission (raised against revision 2, addressed in revision 3): Deetjen also counts only the extra cost of a heat pump over replacing the existing heater with a similar one, assuming the purchase happens when the heater is nearing the end of its life (L499-L506).
